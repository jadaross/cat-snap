# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

## Introduction

CatSnap ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the CatSnap iOS application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information
- **Email address**: Required for account creation and authentication
- **Display name**: Optional username for your profile
- **Profile information**: Optional bio, avatar photo

### 2. Location Data
- **GPS coordinates**: Collected when you submit cat sightings to tag the location where you spotted a cat
- **Approximate location**: Used to show nearby cat sightings on the map

### 3. Photos and User Content
- **Cat sighting photos**: Photos you upload when submitting cat sightings
- **Avatar photos**: Optional profile picture
- **User-generated content**: Cat descriptions, sighting notes, and any text you contribute

### 4. Social Features
- **Friends and follows**: Information about who you follow and who follows you
- **Activity data**: Your cat sighting history and interactions

### 5. Device Information
- **Device identifier**: For authentication and session management
- **App version**: To provide support and improve the app

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the CatSnap service
- Process and display cat sightings on the map
- Enable social features like following friends and seeing their activity
- Authenticate users and secure accounts
- Improve our app and develop new features
- Respond to support requests
- Comply with legal obligations

## Data Storage and Security

- **Database**: Your data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL database)
- **Storage**: Photos are stored in Supabase Storage with appropriate access controls
- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest where applicable
- **Access**: Only authorized personnel and systems can access your data

## Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:
- All your personal information, sightings, photos, and social connections are permanently removed
- Data is deleted from our database and storage within 30 days
- Some data may be retained in backups for a limited time as required by law

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Supabase**: Database, authentication, and storage
- **Apple**: Sign in with Apple authentication
- These services have their own privacy policies and data handling practices

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Delete**: Permanently delete your account and all associated data (use the in-app "Delete account" feature in Settings)
- **Correct**: Update or correct your personal information
- **Export**: Request your data in a portable format

## Children's Privacy

CatSnap is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Posting a notice in the app

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:
- Email: support@catsnap.app
- In-app: Settings → Contact support
